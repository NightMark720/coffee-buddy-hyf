# Ch.7 sound sources

Ch.7 reuses Ch.1's local sound assets. Interface cues originate in the HyperFrames bundled Pixabay library. Liquid, motor, pouring and mechanical textures originate in the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License catalog. Original source URLs, excerpt positions and processing are recorded in sources.json. Audio is retrieved source material, trimmed and mixed for this demo.

typing-ch7.wav is a local arrangement of 48 taps derived from the inherited key.wav cue; it accompanies the 69-character input and is paced independently of individual letters. Other reused cues represent the invention spark, fizz, pouring, orange landing, cup docking, confirmation and save actions.

The motor, extraction, disposing, reset and steam cues are illustrative stock textures; none is a Yeema One recording. There is no narration or music.
