# Ch.2 - One sentence, two cups

## Intent

Continue the Coffee Buddy series in one continuous app conversation. A single natural-language sentence contains two drinks with different serving contexts. Yeema parses the sentence, asks for both cups, then executes the two tasks sequentially without leaving the chat.

## Assets

- STORYBOARD-SOURCE.md - unchanged copy of the supplied Ch.2 storyboard.
- Figma Page 1 - Home Ready (537:1071), talk/texting (537:1074), task status (537:3337), and machine status/workbench (715:4937).
- assets/fonts/ - the series local SF Pro and Inter fonts.
- assets/avatars/ - approved coffee bean assistant and coffee cup user avatars.
- assets/figma/ - copied series status bar and microphone assets.
- assets/sfx/ - local keyboard, send, parsing, extraction, steam, and completion cues copied from the approved series set.

## Customizations

- Independent folder and output identity: coffee-buddy-ch2. Ch.1, Ch.4, and Ch.7 remain untouched.
- 1080 x 1920 portrait, 22 seconds, 30 fps. English app copy, SFX only, no voiceover or music.
- The left black chat avatar is Coffee Buddy as a coffee bean. The right black chat avatar and top profile are the same coffee cup.
- Espresso uses a small cup icon. Latte uses a takeaway cup icon. Both are restrained line icons inside the existing order-card vocabulary.
- Static keyframe review is required before seek-safe animation, audio placement, and MP4 export.
