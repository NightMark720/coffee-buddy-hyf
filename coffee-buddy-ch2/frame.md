## Layout decisions

The fixed status bar, profile, composer and home indicator remain anchored. Conversation content scrolls upward as the understanding pill, order card and brew card arrive. The focus is the two-row order and the serial progress card. No flying objects, scene cuts, decorative background, or physical-machine footage.

## Icon treatment

The espresso and takeaway cup marks are monochrome line icons placed inside the order rows. The espresso icon is compact and low, while the takeaway icon has a taller lid and body. They are secondary to the drink names and do not introduce a new color system.
