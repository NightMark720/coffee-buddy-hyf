# Ch.1 sound sources

Interface cues reuse the HyperFrames bundled Pixabay library. Liquid, motor, pouring and mechanical textures come from the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License catalog. Exact source URLs, excerpt positions and processing are recorded in sources.json. All audio is retrieved source material, trimmed and mixed for this demo.

The motor, extraction, disposing, reset and steam cues are illustrative stock textures; none is a Yeema One recording. There is no narration or music.
